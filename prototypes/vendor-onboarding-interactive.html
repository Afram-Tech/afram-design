🎯 Choosing Your Path

  • Choose Individual if: You're a property owner or independent dealer selling your own properties
  • Choose Company if: You represent a registered company, development firm, or real estate business
  • Switch Anytime: You can change between Individual and Company during registration
  • Both Options: Support seller financing, property listing, and buyer connections
